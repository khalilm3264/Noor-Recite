## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a reverent, tranquil, and intellectually clear environment tailored for Quranic contemplation and auditory recitation. It balances solemn spiritual depth with state-of-the-art acoustic clarity.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Tone:** Reverent, luminous, disciplined, peaceful, and scholarly.
- **Audience:** Everyday reciters, students of tajweed and memorization, and scholarly readers seeking a distraction-free, aesthetically sanctified interface.
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Modern Spiritual Luxury. A fusion of contemporary minimalism with sacred Islamic architectural poise. It replaces heavy, dated ornamentation with restrained geometric framing, micro-embellished dividers, and rhythmic typography.

### Design Principles
1. **Sacred Serenity (Sakina):** Visual noise is eliminated to maintain meditative focus. Whitespace and tonal depth protect the mind from cognitive exhaustion.
2. **Textual Primacy:** The Quranic text occupies absolute visual sovereignty. English translations, tajweed markers, and playback metadata serve as quiet, harmonized companions.
3. **Synchronized Illumination:** Audio-driven interactions evoke radiant luminescence ("Noor") through soft emerald washes and warm amber halos rather than harsh neon alerts.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from historic Quranic manuscript illumination: rich forest and cypress greens, aged and radiant gold leaf, set against cool ivory and crystalline parchment backdrops.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#064E3B`):** Deep Forest Emerald. Anchor for structural navigation, primary app bars, chapter markers, and core active states.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`):** Warm Amber Gold. Used for radiant interaction highlights, active tajweed rules, audio scrub heads, and verse completion seals.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E`):** Persian Teal. Handles auxiliary meta-information, secondary audio cues, translation headers, and subtle geometric tinting.
- **Neutral (`#022C22`):** Night Cypress. Used as the anchor for deep contrast text in light mode and foundation surface layering in dark mode.

### Functional Roles & Contrast
- **Canvas & Surface (Light Mode):** Background is `#FBFBFA` (warm alabaster parchment) with card tiers layered in `#FFFFFF`. Muted dividers use `#E2E8F0` with a 20% emerald wash.
- **Canvas & Surface (Dark Mode):** Base canvas is `#022C22` transitioning to `#064E3B` (at 20% opacity over `#0B1E19`) for elevated verse cards, maintaining deep contrast without blinding glare.
- **Active Synchronized Recitation:** 
  - Word chip base: `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`.
  - Word chip active/playing: `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)` background with a crisp border stroke of `#D97706` and an amber-gold ambient glow (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`).

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is bi-directional and dual-script: classical Arabic calligraphy rules govern the Quranic text, while modern Latin geometric typography delivers crystalline translations, tajweed guides, and control labels.

### Typographic Roles
- **Arabic Text (Amiri / Scheherazade New):** Requires generous vertical line clearance (`lineHeight` nearly 2x the `fontSize`) to accommodate complex diacritics (harakat, sukun, shaddah) and tajweed stops without vertical clipping.
- **Latin Translation & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly, wide apertures and crisp readability at compact sizes. It offsets the heavy flourishes of the Arabic script with geometric clarity.
- **Transliteration:** Rendered with italicized weighting and gentle tracking to clearly distinguish phonetic study text from standard commentary.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an 8pt vertical grid combined with a single-column fluid card layout for mobile touchscreens, expanding to a stabilized max-width container on larger viewport formats.

### Layout Model
- **Reading Canvas:** The reading view adheres strictly to a centered safe reading channel (max 680px width) ensuring optimal saccadic eye movement across both Arabic right-to-left (RTL) reading and Latin left-to-right (LTR) translation tracks.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout. Margin of `1rem`, gutter of `1rem`. Floating bottom player dock with compact action tray.
  - **Tablet / Split View (640px – 1024px):** Dual-pane capabilities (Ayah reader left, tafsir/lexicon right). Margin expands to `1.5rem`.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Fixed reading frame centered at 720px with auxiliary recitation drawers on either side.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical padding within Ayah cards utilizes `space-lg` to create rhythmic breathing space between the sacred Arabic script, the pronunciation guide, and the English meaning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via gentle, illuminated layering rather than harsh industrial drop-shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Parchment Base):** `#FBFBFA` flat background.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Ayah Card):** Pure white surface bounded by a subtle `1px` stroke of `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.06)`. Diffused ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Active Recitation Card):** Elevated using an amber-tinted shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, framed by a delicate border in `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Media Deck & Sheets):** Frosted glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with a `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` base fill in light mode or `rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.82)` in dark mode, supported by a 1px top highlight stroke.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette balances architectural structure with physical comfort in hand. 

### Corner Radius System
- **Level 2 (Default - 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard interactive elements including input fields, audio control buttons, and dropdown menus.
- **Large (1rem / 16px):** Primary Ayah cards, modal dialogs, and tafsir panels.
- **Pill (Full Rounding):** Word-by-word synchronized chips, bookmark tags, tajweed rule indicator badges, and audio scrub thumbs.
- **Islamic Geometry Accents:** Surah header banners and card divider terminals feature discreet 8-point star (Rub el Hizb) micro-framing and stepped geometric filigree.

## Components

### 1. Ayah Card
- **Structure:** Vertical container with Surah/Ayah badge header, sacred Arabic text block, transliteration text, and English translation block.
- **Resting State:** White container, 16px corner radius, hairline green-slate border.
- **Active Recitation State:** Left-edge accent spine (3px solid `#D97706`), soft emerald background gradient wash (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(6,78,59,0.02) 0%, rgba(217,119,6,0.03) 100%)`).

### 2. Synchronized Word Pills
- **Nature:** High-precision touch targets wrapping each Arabic word for recitation tracking.
- **Inactive:** Transparent background, zero border, comfortable tap target (min 44px vertical height).
- **Active / Vocalized:** Soft rounded pill shape with `0.25rem` padding, filled with `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`, bounded by a `1.5px` border in `#D97706`, accompanied by an ambient gold text glow (`text-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`).

### 3. Audio Waveform & Player Deck
- **Container:** Floating bar anchored above the bottom navigation bar with 20px margin, featuring 24px corner radius and glassmorphism backdrop blur.
- **Waveform:** Dynamic amplitude bars colored in `#0F766E` for unplayed tracks, transitioning to `#D97706` for played duration. Scrub head is a warm gold pill with current timestamp tooltip.
- **Playback Controls:** Primary Play/Pause is a 56px circle in deep emerald (`#064E3B`) with a warm gold icon (`#FEF3C7`). Secondary controls (rewind 5s, repeat ayah, tajweed speed) use subtle ghost icons.

### 4. Surah & Juz Navigation Chips
- **Style:** Compact pill chips featuring dual labels (Arabic name + English transliteration).
- **Default State:** Alabaster fill, 1px border in `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.1)`.
- **Selected State:** Solid `#064E3B` fill, `#FFFFFF` typography, with a `#D97706` dot indicator.

### 5. Tajweed & Lexicon Cards
- **Display:** Slide-up bottom sheets utilizing `rounded-xl` top edges. Tonal color tags (crimson for ghunnah, cobalt for qalqalah, amber for idgham) applied as soft translucent pill badges.
