# Noor Recite

Noor Recite brings Quran reading, recorded recitation, memorization practice, and Tajweed study into one responsive app.

## Start here

- [Getting started and deployment](https://github.com/khalilm3264/Noor-Recite/wiki/Getting-Started): install, configure, run, and troubleshoot.
- [User guide](https://github.com/khalilm3264/Noor-Recite/wiki/User-Guide): reading, audio, bookmarks, Hifz, and practice.
- [AI and privacy](https://github.com/khalilm3264/Noor-Recite/wiki/AI-and-Privacy): what is sent to Gemini, what stays in the browser, and service limits.
- [Architecture and sources](https://github.com/khalilm3264/Noor-Recite/wiki/Architecture-and-Sources): project structure and external dependencies.

## Scope

Tajweed feedback is a practice aid, not a certification of correct recitation. The Warsh option is a limited text preview covering only 1:4 and 2:9; it does not supply a complete Warsh Muṣḥaf or switch the reader's reciter.

The reader can start without a Gemini key, but additional chapters, recorded audio, translations, and fonts may need internet access. This is not a fully offline app.
