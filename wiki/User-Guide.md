# User guide

## Read and listen

Open **Read** for the reader or **Surahs** for the chapter directory. Select a chapter, choose a recorded reciter in **Preferences**, and use the playback controls. Select a word to explore its meaning and study information.

Preferences control fonts, translation display, audio choices, and reading presentation. Use the theme button for light parchment or night cypress mode. On a phone, the main sections appear in the bottom navigation.

Recorded reciter audio is distinct from AI-generated speech used by some study features. Loading additional chapters, audio, translations, and fonts can require internet access.

## Save and review

Use bookmarks and collections to return to passages. Daily goals, reading history, and memorization progress are stored in this browser. They are not synchronized through an account. Clearing browser storage or switching browsers can remove or separate that progress.

## Memorization practice

**Hifz Quiz** includes word completion, blind recall, next-ayah recall, and voice drills. Review weak words and use the spaced-review queue to revisit material. Treat scores as practice feedback; they do not certify memorization or correct recitation.

## Tajweed studio

Open **Tajweed** to study rule examples and practice. Listen to the reference, record only when you choose to, and compare your attempt. The browser will request microphone permission for recording.

Voice verification sends the submitted recording to the server and Gemini. If verification is unavailable, the self-test cannot mark the recording as verified. Voice activity and duration checks cannot establish correct makhraj, nasalization, or every Tajweed rule; seek feedback from a qualified teacher.

## Qira'at and text previews

The comparison module contains labeled sample audio and transmission information. The reader's Warsh setting is only a limited text preview: **1:4 and 2:9** are adjusted; other verses keep the base Ḥafṣ text. It does not switch the reader's reciter or provide a complete Warsh Muṣḥaf.

## Prayer and study sections

Use **Salah & Prayers**, **Duas & Kalimas**, and **Popular Hadiths** for their respective study material. Check references and report specific content errors with a reliable source. AI-generated pronunciation or translation may need correction.
